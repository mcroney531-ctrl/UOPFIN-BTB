## 1. Minimum viable intervention — four materials

| Material | Who uses it | What it is | Locked targets served |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Lead calibration kit** | Pilot Leads | One-page performance model (the six Quality fields + stop the restart loop + explain accurately); 2–3 de-identified clips (Elias offered, Oct 1) showing restart loop vs. serial-first vs. ineligible; Ben's recognition cue with Imani's "impact first"; **diagnostic questions** for a broken call — what did the Guide hear, what did they do next, and was it understanding, tool access, or a decision? | 2, 1, 5 |
| **B. Guide practice huddle** | Pilot Leads run it with their Guides | ~15 min, Imani's tested format (Sep 18): two clips → each Guide writes a one-sentence transition from listening to action → "what action does it unlock?" → three short scenarios: **eligible, ineligible, pending Plus** | 1, 2 |

The sequence with the *why* of each step; both paths; what to say about Plus (Nina, Sep 21; Specialist wording, Sep 12); what to do if Plus is pending or fails (Sep 9 rule + Sep 24 model call); recap and notes checklist (confirmation number, status of each transaction, follow-up) | 3, 1 |

**Not in the MVI:** an all-Guide video, a course, a manager course, a Sana module for Guides. A short model-call video could be cut from Elias's clips later if the pilot shows it helps — optional, not the core.

## 3. Evidence of success

**Compare pilot teams with non-pilot General Care teams over the same two weeks** — this controls for things that change for everyone, such as the proactive notice going out (Jules, Oct 1).

| Observe | Source | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Of eligible calls with a serial check: appropriate path taken | Call sample / Orbit | The 44% gap (G1) |
| Recovery complete, or follow-up opened when Plus is pending | Orbit fields + Quality sample | G2; Marisol's "complete recovery" |
| Plus explained accurately; clear recap | Quality listening | G4; Marisol's "a recovery customers believe" |
| Ineligible callers get ordinary support, no promise | Quality listening | Guardrail: no "every WN7 is a replacement" |
| Repeat contacts on the same case | Orbit | Marisol, Oct 1 |
| Lead can coach from the cue (Lead self-report + Quality observation) | Lead debrief | Target 2 |
| AHT, wait, abandonment | Daily operations | Operational checks, not promised savings (Renee, Marisol) |
| Customer feedback on pilot recoveries | Feedback (voluntary) | Directional only |

- **Expand if:** pilot teams show better path selection and complete-or-followed-up recoveries than non-pilot teams, with no rise in repeat contacts, and Renee judges the queue impact acceptable. Thresholds set with Quality and Workforce — not invented by Learning.
- **Revise if:** serial checks still don't turn into action → investigate tool access and screen reading before more coaching; pending-Plus confusion persists → escalate the partner decision; ineligible callers get promised replacements → strengthen that scenario.
- **Stop or rethink if:** Leads can't run the huddle or listen within their time, or queue impact is unacceptable.
- **Claim level:** "promising" or "not yet," never proof of cause (Elias, Sep 30 and Oct 1).

## Where to pick this apart

1. **Pilot size** — 2 Leads, 4, or something else? Include Specialist Leads?
2. **Three vs. four centers** — one Lead per center assumes four.
3. **Lead session length** — 30 min is a guess; Priya and Imani suggest smaller.
4. **Sana** — a short Lead readiness check (item 26), or leave it out of the MVI?
5. **Video** — fully out, or a short model-call clip inside A/B?
6. **Two weeks** — long enough to see anything, short enough for Dana's "not November"?
7. **What you'd build for the interview** — C alone, B + C, or all four in light form?
8. **Lead WN7 knowledge (item 66)** — kit A assumes Leads know the WN7 well enough to coach it; the packet has no baseline and the evidence is mixed. Does Lead preparation need a knowledge layer, a quick check, or neither?
