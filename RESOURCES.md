## R1. Lead one-pager

- **Audience:** Team Leads only (a Guide-facing reference, if any, is separate).
- **When used:** handed out at the end of the Lead alignment meeting; pulled up before a huddle, when a Guide puts a customer on hold and asks for help, or while reviewing a call.
- **Job:** preserve the alignment the meeting builds and help Leads put it into practice. The meeting builds shared understanding; the one-pager keeps it.
- **Structure (yours):**
  1. Here's the situation
  2. What you need to know
  3. How you can help your Guides
  4. What to do afterward
- **Possible contents (GPT suggestions, not decided):** what Guides need to know (failure pattern, eligibility limits, recovery actions, Plus); what good looks like; diagnostic coaching questions (misunderstanding vs. tool vs. execution); what to escalate (Orbit access, pending Plus, policy questions).
- **Constraints:** one page; practical, light on instruction; point to the Guide reference rather than restate it.
- **Open:** contents not yet drafted.

## R2. Team Lead working meeting

- **Length:** 20 minutes. Planning assumption, subject to Workforce approval.
- **Agenda (decided):**
  - 3 min — Situation and WN7 refresher (hard stop at 3; deeper questions go to the cases or follow-up)
  - 7 min — Case discussion and understanding check
  - 7 min — Align on Guide expectations and Lead coaching
  - 3 min — Takeaway (R1), responsibilities, next steps — includes the coordination thread (R3) and asking Leads to introduce the video (R4) in their next huddle
- **Job:** build shared understanding, surface misunderstandings and operational blockers, calibrate what good looks like and how to coach it.
- **Open:** which cases fit in the 7 minutes; who attends from Quality and Specialist Care.

- **One thread for all Leads** (not per small group). Meeting establishes alignment · one-pager (R1) preserves it · thread maintains it.
- **Scope:** questions, blockers, useful coaching observations, confirmed updates — not a channel Leads must constantly monitor.
- **Beacon stays the official source** of procedural guidance.

- **Open:** owner and verifier; where it lives; how verified updates are marked.

- **Audience:** Guides.
- **Delivery:** email plus Leads mentioning it in their huddles; Leads keep it on hand for coaching and point Guides to it when the difficulty shows up. Email alone isn't treated as delivered.
- **Job:** close the gap between checking the serial and acting on the result.
- **Possible focus (GPT, not decided):** when to check · what to look for in the Orbit panel · what happens next (Eligible → recovery; not eligible → ordinary support; link to `BH-4419`).

- **Status:** decided, conditional on the Orbit screen and General Care access being verified.
- **Complements R1:** R1 helps Leads coach; R4 shows Guides the tool. Neither restates `BH-4419`.
