## 2. Core performance standard (draft)

Quality's six draft fields, Sep 24 (item 52): **recognition of the pattern · serial eligibility checked · appropriate path selected · replacement complete when eligible · Plus credit complete or follow-up opened · clear recap.**

| Sienna's stage (item 51) | Behaviors | Not explicit in the six fields |
|---|---|---|
| Find and recognize | Recognize the pattern; locate guidance ([GPT] addition) | — |
| Apply and decide | Check serial eligibility; select recovery or ordinary path | Stop the redundant restart loop (Elias, item 44; Dana, item 5) |
| Execute and close | Both transactions or follow-up opened; clear recap | Conversation quality beyond recap — "a concrete acknowledgment and a useful pivot" (Sienna, item 33); notes for the next contact (item 53) |

- Use first for coaching and learning from exceptions, not as a score (Priya, Elias, item 52).
- Score conditionally: ineligible ≠ failure; pending handled correctly ≠ failure (Malik, items 33, 43).
- **Fixed sequence, flexible words:** "it is a sequence, not a speech" (Harper, item 41).
