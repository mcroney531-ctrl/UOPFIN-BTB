One call, three decisions

The call so far

Customer“Every few days the Wi-Fi drops for the whole house for a minute or two, then it comes back by itself. It's a WN7 hub.”

Customer“My friend has the same hub and Northstar sent them a new one. I want a replacement too. This keeps knocking me off work calls.”

WN7 eligibility: Not eligible

1Moment 1 of 3 · Recognize and verify

What do you know right now?

What do the symptoms tell you, and what does the Orbit result establish?

Compare with the model response

Your response

Model response

  • The symptoms fit the WN7 pattern, so they were the right trigger to check eligibility.
  • Orbit says Not eligible for this serial. That result decides the path.
  • The model, the symptoms and the friend's experience don't change it. No replacement or Plus benefit is on offer through this path.

Why this matters: recognizing the pattern means check, not qualifies. The model code alone doesn't establish eligibility; only the serial result does.

Source: PO-117 via BH-4419 (“eligible only when Orbit returns Eligible… The model code alone does not establish eligibility”).

2Moment 2 of 3 · Decide

They still want a replacement

Customer“So you're not going to replace it? It's the same hub my friend had.”

What do you do next, and why?

Compare with the model response

Your response

Model response

  • Acknowledge the disruption to their work calls. Don't brush it off.
  • Continue the appropriate standard troubleshooting, or escalation, for the drops they're reporting, using the standard Beacon guidance.
  • Don't promise a replacement or the Plus benefit, and don't hint that one might come later.
  • If the issue continues after standard support, follow the established escalation for the reported problem.

Why this matters: the drops are real even though this hub isn't eligible, and other causes of whole-home drops exist. They still need help, just not the recovery path. A product name isn't a promise.

Source: PO-117 (“If Orbit returns Not eligible, continue appropriate troubleshooting or escalation for the reported issue. Do not promise the replacement or free service year”); Sep 24 notes.

3Moment 3 of 3 · Explain and document

Close the loop

What would you tell the customer, and what would you note for the next Guide?

Compare with the model response

Your response

A strong answer covers

  • Impact: acknowledge that the drops are disrupting their work.
  • Result: their hub's serial isn't in the group covered by the replacement, so you can't offer one through this path. Don't say every WN7 is affected.
  • Next steps: only what's confirmed, such as the troubleshooting you're doing now or the escalation you've opened.
  • Notes: the eligibility result, what you tried, and any open follow-up with its owner.

Why this matters: in the documented call, the Guide said she couldn't offer a replacement through this path and recapped the next step. Clear notes mean the next Guide doesn't have to rebuild what happened. Check that the customer understands the next step.

Source: Sep 24 listening notes; PO-117 (“Do not say every WN7 is affected”). Use your own words; this isn't an approved script.

Wrap-up

Before your next WN7 call

  1. What cue told you to check eligibility?
  2. What did the result establish, and what didn't it?
  3. What will you do on a real call when Orbit says Not eligible?

Keep the WN7 Call Path handy on calls. If you're unsure on a live call, ask your Lead.

Finishing this practice shows you took part. It doesn't measure how you handle live calls; your Lead looks at that through normal call reviews and coaching.

Optional variation

Same call, but Orbit won't load

The customer describes the same drops. You have the serial, but the WN7 eligibility panel won't load.

What do you do, and what do you tell the customer?

Compare with the model response

Your response

Model response

  • Don't guess eligibility either way.
  • Ask your Lead if you're unsure what to do.
  • If the tool stays unavailable, open an eligibility review case as BH-4419 describes.
  • Tell the customer what happens next, without promising a time you can't confirm.

Why this matters: a guess either way creates a wrong promise or a missed recovery.

Source: PO-117 (“If the serial cannot be located or the tool is unavailable, open an eligibility review case… Do not guess”). The follow-up expectation for a review case still needs Product Ops confirmation.
